Grogu privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Grogu is a private personal assistant used only by its owner. It is not available to other users.

What Grogu accesses

When the owner connects their Google account, Grogu requests read-only access to that account's Gmail (gmail.readonly) plus basic sign-in identity (email address). It cannot send, draft, label, delete, or otherwise change mail.

How the data is used

  • Mail is read only to give the owner reminders, summaries, and alerts about their own messages.
  • Credentials are stored in the owner's macOS Keychain on the owner's own computer.
  • Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with or transferred to third parties, except as needed to provide these features to the owner (for example, sending a portion of a message to the owner's own self-hosted language model for summarization).
  • Humans other than the owner do not read the data.

Grogu's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
